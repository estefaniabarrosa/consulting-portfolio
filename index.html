CASE STUDY / 01

Brazil · Remote
2025 · Approx. 6 months

Audiovisual Production Company

Website strategy, content planning & paid media performance · Audiovisual production

Meta AdsAudience segmentationMetricoolWebsite UX & CTAsPerformance reporting

The challenge

The company needed a clearer website, consistent marketing activity and a better understanding of which channels were attracting potential customers. Paid media budgets were limited.

What I did

  • Created the website blueprint, page copy, navigation and calls to action for the developer and UX designer.
  • Planned monthly content, developed creative briefs and supported publishing across social platforms.
  • Built and managed Meta Ads campaigns, including audience segmentation, creative A/B testing and budget allocation within the owners' approved spend.
  • Analyzed Metricool and Meta Ads reports, compared reach, clicks, CTR and CPC, and used campaign findings to guide testing and recommend better lead tracking.
What I delivered: Website structure and UX recommendations, monthly editorial planning, Meta Ads setup and audience segmentation, A/B testing, budget management, and performance reports with actionable recommendations.
CASE STUDY / 02

Artisanal home fragrance · Small business

Independent Home Fragrance Brand

E-commerce assessment & commercial analysis · Artisanal candles

Shopify sales analysisCustomer behaviorSales channelsData limitations

The challenge

The business relied heavily on Instagram and offline markets. Its available digital sales records did not capture all activity, making it harder to assess performance across channels.

What I did

  • Reviewed recorded Shopify orders and revenue to identify differences between direct online orders and manually recorded sales.
  • Examined the customer contact list and the gaps in tracking offline market sales, costs and website traffic.
  • Recommended clearer sales recording, a stronger customer journey and a website-led funnel to support future decisions.
What I delivered: A sales performance assessment using available e-commerce data, identification of tracking gaps, and practical recommendations for customer journeys and sales reporting. Offline revenue was not fully recorded; no total business results are claimed.
CASE STUDY / 03

Portugal · Confidential client

Independent Tax Advisory Consultancy

Digital launch, content strategy & lead generation foundations · Professional services

Audience segmentationInstagram launchWhatsApp BusinessContent strategy

The challenge

A tax adviser serving Spanish-speaking residents in Portugal needed to establish her digital presence and explain specialist services in a way potential clients could understand.

What I did

  • Helped launch the Instagram account and set up WhatsApp Business as a direct enquiry channel.
  • Defined the audience, communication themes and content plan around the tax season.

  • Recommended a future website and lead generation improvements as possible next steps.
What I delivered: Initial digital channels, messaging and a repeatable content process. No client revenue or lead conversion figures are claimed. The business identity is withheld for confidentiality.
